# Zork II on brass-lantern: gap survey

Sources: `/tmp/zork2-src` (2dungeon.zil, 2actions.zil, g*.zil), story `COMPILED/zork2.z3`; engine at `brass-lantern` (main, after 1.12.5). Read-only survey.

## 1. Summary

**Scale (ZIL counts):**

| | Zork I | Zork II |
|---|---|---|
| Rooms | 110 | 86 (9 are the Oddly-angled maze, 4 the post-death mist rooms, 4 volcano air rooms) |
| Objects (dungeon file) | ~140 | 147 (story file: 251 objects in total) |
| Actors (ACTORBIT) | troll, thief, cyclops, bat, ghosts | wizard, demon, robot, dragon, princess, unicorn, Cerberus, sea serpent, volcano gnome, Gnome of Zurich, plus the lizard door-keeper (not ACTORBIT) and global stand-ins for the unicorn, princess and Cerberus |
| Interrupts | ~12 | 23 (I-WIZARD, I-SPELL, I-WAND, I-DRAGON, I-GARDEN, I-PRINCESS, I-UNICORN, I-BALLOON, I-BURNUP, I-FUSE, I-SAFE, I-LEDGE, I-GNOME, I-NERVOUS, I-ZGNOME, I-ZGNOME-OUT, I-CURTAIN, I-SPHERE, I-BUCKET, I-SPRAY, I-MATCH, I-LANTERN, I-SWORD) |
| Score | 350 | **400**: 17 treasures taken (330, SCORE-OBJ zeroes VALUE), 5 rooms entered (35: Cerberus 10, Workshop 10, Top of Well 10, Anteroom 3, Crypt 2), riddle, diamond maze and dragon (5 each), demon gifts (2 each, up to 10) |
| Melee combat | yes | **none**: V-ATTACK with a weapon prints “You can’t.” Every fight is a rule. |
| Thief | yes | **none**. The wizard is the random wanderer instead. |

**Fidelity baseline:** the shipped `zork2.z3` is byte-identical (md5 `e3fd4c0a…`) to `zork2.zip` in the source snapshot and to `brass-lantern/public/stories/zork2.z3`: Release 63, Serial 860811. Its object tree has 86 rooms, which matches the source. I found no source/story disagreement, but I didn't do a string-level check (my ad-hoc Z-string decoder dropped some strings).

**Headline gaps.** Nothing structural is missing the way combat or vehicles were for Zork I. Zork II's difficulty sits in four places:
1. **Characters who carry out orders.** “ROBOT, PUSH TRIANGULAR BUTTON”, “ROBOT, LIFT CAGE” and “DEMON, GIVE ME THE WAND” are on the critical path. `handleOrder` in `src/engine/verbs/talk.ts` is documented as “No one obeys yet.”
2. **Script/API plumbing** that the many state-machine puzzles need: resolve words to IDs, evaluate conditions, list passable exits, and read literal words (SAY FLOAT, ANSWER WELL).
3. **The wizard and his 12 spells**, which are global modifiers on the player (MAGIC-ACTOR becomes the ADVENTURER's action routine). These are mostly script work through a world `capture`, plus two small engine hooks (carry limit and fumble odds from variables).
4. **Fidelity details** that a differential test would catch: one interrupt queue in Zork's order, selective KILL-INTERRUPTS on death, RANDOMIZE-OBJECTS into a container, the SCORE and DIAGNOSE texts, computed room and item descriptions, and MUNG-ROOM.

## 3. Category B: world scripts needed

**The wizard and spells** (largest script):
- I-WIZARD: a self-requeuing fuse (4 turns, then 5+RANDOM(30−5·spheres)). It vanishes, appears in four texts (dark, giant in Posts/Pool, floating, normal), flees the black sphere, fizzles, and casts RANDOM(12) spells. It also handles Fluoresce (ALWAYS-LIT, via `darkness.litIf` on a flag) and arrives in the Pentagram room when the demon is loose.

## 4. Category A: notable confirmations

- **Map:** `exits` with `if` for `IF FLAG` (SECRET-DOOR, ICE-MELTED, CERBERUS-LEASHED, MENHIR-POSITION, GNOME-DOOR-FLAG, DIM-DOOR-FLAG), `door` for `IF X IS OPEN` (crypt, wizard, oak and riddle doors), message exits, and `denials`. LAND and CROSS are Zork II directions: exits labelled `land`/`cross` plus world verbs with `go: true`.
- **Objects:** containers and surfaces (`container`, `surface`: workbench, stands, tables, receptacle, chest, keyholes), `hide`/`reveal` for INVISIBLE (bat club, iron box, candy, dim door), `unvisit` for clearing TOUCHBIT, `scenery` for LOCAL-GLOBALS. GLOBAL-OBJECTS (wish, walls, compass, footpad, tunnel) go in every room's `scenery`, generated in TS.
- **Scoring:** room VALUE becomes `onEnter` scored flags; treasures are `took_*` flags or `score` effects; SCORE-UPD amounts use `{ score }`; `maxScore: 400`.
- **Light and death:** darkness, grue and `stumble`/`blunder` with the repellent as `{ if: 'flag:sprayed' }` (“sinister gurgling noises”); `darkness.litIf` for Fluoresce; death respawn at Room of Red Mist with `variants.before` setting `dead`; DEAD-PALANTIR-4 `onEnter` testing `var:deaths>=3` (the engine counts `vars.deaths`) or the demon loose, giving an `endings` entry or a revival to the barrow.
- **Characters:** `npcs` with `holds`, `descriptions`, `instead.give` (any treasure via a rule script reading `ctx.treasure`) and `refuseOrder`. No `combat` block is needed: Zork II has no melee, and ATTACK falls to rules.
- **Verbs:** world verbs for WAVE…AT, SPRAY…ON, TIE…TO, UNTIE, MELT, PLAY, KISS, WAKE/ALARM, KNOCK, MAKE (wish), FOLLOW, LOOK UNDER/BEHIND, WALK THROUGH, RAISE/LIFT, HELLO. Multi-word world phrases parse before built-ins.
- **Vehicles and time:** vehicle `onEnd` and `instead` for the bucket and the balloon (asked before the room's); fuses and daemons for every I-* with a variable timer where Zork requeues; a world `capture` for MAGIC-ACTOR (it also sees AGAIN and LLM readings).

## 5. Parser, syntax and verb differences from Zork I

- **gparser.zil and gmain.zil are effectively identical** to Zork I's (diffs are commented-out MOBY-FIND, FIND-NOT-HERE and PRSO-PRINT). gsyntax.zil is the shared trilogy file; the Zork II branches are:
  - BUZZ FEEBLE … FANTASIZE FROTZ OZMOO: spell words are noise to the parser and read raw by V-INCANT and V-SAY.
  - ATTACK/KILL OBJECT with no weapon goes to V-ATTACK.
- **New directions:** LAND and CROSS (`<DIRECTIONS … LAND CROSS>`).
- **Orders are carried out** (gap #1): V-TELL sets WINNER and HERE to the actor's room, and the next command resets WINNER unless QUOTE-FLAG. Every verb runs with that winner: GOTO prints “The robot leaves the room.”, ITAKE moves things to the robot (and SCORE-OBJ fires!), JIGS-UP kills the actor.
- **Verbs that only Zork II objects answer** (defaults in Zork I): INCANT/CHANT, SAY, ANSWER/REPLY (raw words), ENCHANT and DISENCHANT (internal PERFORMs), PUT/SLIDE X UNDER Y, READ X WITH/THROUGH Y, WAVE X AT Y, RUB X WITH Y, SPRAY X ON Y, TIE X TO Y and UNTIE, MELT, MAKE, FOLLOW, WAKE, KISS, PLAY, KNOCK, CLIMB ON (bucket), THROUGH (walls, curtain, crack, pool), LOOK UNDER/BEHIND.
- **Replaced verbs:** V-SCORE (“would be”, “This score gives you…”, the 400 ranks are G? thresholds 40/80/160/240/320/360, so `min` is threshold+1) and V-DIAGNOSE (spells and deaths, no wounds). V-BURN gains the receptacle branch; V-THROUGH gains the bank SCOL branches; GOTO gains RMUNGBIT, the balloon landing line, SPRAYED?, and per-actor messages.

## 6. Risks for differential testing against zork2.z3

- **The wizard is everywhere.** I-WIZARD fires from move 4, every 4 turns, at 10% to appear, then nested PROB 80/20/50/50, RANDOM(12) spells and random durations, with no thief-style lair to avoid. Expect to pin seed pairs per chapter on both sides (`ZMachineSession` `{ seed }` already exists) and to add sync points or line-set filtering for wizard lines. It's the analogue of Zork I's thief, but it changes player state (spells), so a stray cast diverges the whole run.
- **Other random sites:** the carousel (80% until flipped), the diamond maze (PROB 33/25, RANDOM 4/5), magnet room 50/50, topiary 12/8/4%, unicorn 33/25%, dragon 50/25%, princess step timing 75/25, the chest 25%, Cerberus 50%, robot PROB 2 and 80/20, the wand 5%, wizard door 5/20%, wizard's quarters text, aquarium shadow lines 25/5%, the palantir view 25%, FANTASIZE 20%, the grue 80%, HELLOS, YUKS and DUMMY. RANDOMIZE-OBJECTS is **deterministic** in Zork II, which makes deaths easy to compare.
- **Timing-sensitive interrupts:** the balloon's 3-turn cycle against BLOC (land or tie at the right moment), I-BURNUP (SIZE×20), the fuse 2, safe 5 and ledge 8 chain, gnome 10 then nervous 5, curtain 12, Zurich gnome 3 and 12, sphere gas 6, bucket 100, spray 8, spell 10–19, wand 2, princess 1–2 per step. These are all exact counts once seeded; the risk is engine/Zork ordering on turns where several fire (gap #6) and WAIT's early stop.
- **Death clears all fuses in the engine but not in Zork** (gap #7). A death mid-balloon or mid-wizard-cycle diverges.
- **Object order:** the story file's room and object order differs from the source's. No wanderer walks rooms in object order here, but listings (newest-first, PRINT-CONT) and RANDOMIZE-OBJECTS' iteration follow the object tree. Reuse `tests/helpers/zobjects.ts` for order.
- **Long walkthrough:** the lamp runs about 650 lit turns, then the wizard's Fluoresce rescue (score>200) changes later lighting. A full-run test must land on the same side of that.

## 7. Open questions

1. Does Release 63 really print “Your score **would be** …” for the SCORE verb (V-SCORE's `ASK?` defaults to T)? Check against the story file before building gap #9.
2. I-WIZARD is `<ENABLE <QUEUE I-WIZARD 4>>` in GO but returns early while `,DEAD`. Is its first possible appearance at move 4 or 5 under CLOCKER's decrement-then-fire? This decides the seed hunt.
3. IN-CAGE-FCN does `SETG HERE ,CAGE-ROOM` when CAGE-SOLVE-FLAG is set (no GOTO). Is that observable (status line, LOOK)?
4. How should “ME” resolve (SPRAY REPELLENT ON ME, “demon, give me the wand”, “demon, attack me”)? I couldn't find a self object in the engine's matcher; it may need a scenery “me” item everywhere.
5. GLOBAL-PRINCESS and GLOBAL-UNICORN are ACTORBIT objects in global or local-global scope (“There is no princess here.”, “Unicorn? What unicorn?”). The engine has no global NPCs; are scenery items with rules enough wherever the parser's FIND ACTORBIT syntaxes (GIVE TO, ATTACK, KISS) matter?
6. How far should the wand port go: every spell on every object (FLOAT, FRY, FILCH and FLUORESCE on any TAKEBIT item), or only the puzzle uses (menhir, Cerberus, collar, case) with an allowlist for the rest?
7. Should FANTASIZE's phantom lines and the Fluoresce-any-item light (gaps #4 and #13) be built or allowlisted? Both are random or rare and player-driven.
8. Order continuation: after “robot, go east”, does the rest of a compound line (“… . push button”) stay with the robot? QUOTE-FLAG governs it; I haven't confirmed the behaviour in the story file.
