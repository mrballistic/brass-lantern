# Zork III on brass-lantern: gap survey

Sources: `/tmp/zork3-src` (release files only, per `zork3.zil`: GSYNTAX, GMACROS, GCLOCK, GMAIN, GPARSER, 3DUNGEON, GGLOBALS, GVERBS, 3ACTIONS, with `<SET REDEFINE T>` so 3ACTIONS' `JIGS-UP`, `V-SCORE`, `V-DIAGNOSE`, `LIGHT-INT` override the shared ones). The unprefixed `actions/dungeon/main/parser/verbs/syntax/clock/demons/macros/shadow/tm.zil` are older drafts; `shadow.zil` and `tm.zil` became the "old SHADOW.ZIL" and "old TM.ZIL" sections of 3ACTIONS and weren't needed here.

Story file: `COMPILED/zork3.z3` is Release 25, serial 860811. It is byte-identical to `zork3.zip` in the source dir, and `zork3.record` shows that build compiling exactly the nine files above (3ACTIONS.ZIL.30, 3DUNGEON.ZIL.4 and so on). So the source can be trusted to match the story file closely. I spotted no disagreement, but I didn't decompile anything.

Engine read: brass-lantern `src/types/world.ts`, `src/engine/{engine,rules,scripts,conditions,describe,effects}.ts`, `src/engine/verbs/{movement,attack,talk,world-verbs,meta}.ts`, `src/worlds/zork1.ts`, `docs/guide/porting-zork.md`, backlog and stage specs. (The brass-lantern repo has no ARCHITECTURE.md or WORLDS.md. The equivalents are `docs/guide/*.md` and `docs/reference/*.md`.)

## 1. Summary

**Scale.**

| | Zork III | Zork I |
|---|---|---|
| `<ROOM>`s | 89 (35 in 3DUNGEON, 54 in 3ACTIONS) | 110 |
| `<OBJECT>`s | 121 game + 18 gglobals (story header: 228 objects incl. rooms) | 122 + 18 |
| Vocabulary | 600 words | — |
| Interrupts | 24 (`I-*`) | ~20 |
| Score | 7 points of "potential" | 350 |

The *room count overstates the map*:
- 8 rooms are era duplicates for time travel: `MID-CP-ANTE`, `MID-CP-OUT`, `MID-MUSEUM-ENTRANCE`, `MID-JEWEL-ROOM`, `MID-TECH-MUSEUM`, `OLD-MUSEUM-ENTRANCE`, `OLD-JEWEL-ROOM`, `OLD-TECH-MUSEUM`.
- 8 are vignettes reachable only for 3 turns through the Scenic Vista: `TIMBER-ROOM`, `LADDER-TOP/BOTTOM`, `DEAD-END-5`, `LOWER-SHAFT`, `MACHINE-ROOM`, `ROOM-8`, `ZORK-IV`.
- 17 are the mirror hallway.
- `CP` is one room standing for a 6×6 grid of cells.

Zork III is smaller than Zork I in places, but far more **procedural**. Nearly every puzzle is a state machine over globals (`MDIR`, `MLOC`, `CPHERE`, the `CPTABLE` grid, `YEAR`/`TM-YEAR`, `PNUMB`/`LCELL`, `S-STRENGTH`/`P-STRENGTH`), with exits computed by `PER` routines and room text computed by `M-LOOK`.

**Regions:**
- Endless Stair, Junction, Barren Area
- Land of Shadow (`SHADOW-1..8`, Creepy Crawl, Foggy Room, Flathead Ocean)
- Cliff (Hairpin Loop, Cliff, Cliff Ledge, Cliff Base)
- Lake and aqueduct (Lake Shore, Aqueduct View, On the Lake, Underwater, Western Shore, Scenic Vista, Southern Shore, `DARK-1/2`, Key Room, `AQ-1..3`, Damp Passage, Dead End)
- Engravings Room (old man, secret door)
- Royal Museum (Tight Squeeze, Crystal Grotto, Royal Hall, Great Door, Museum Entrance/Jewel Room/Technology Museum ×3 eras, Royal Puzzle)
- The endgame: Button Room, Beam Room, hallway `MRA..MRD`/`MRG` plus 10 Narrow Rooms, Inside Mirror, Dungeon Entrance, corridors, Parapet, the three cell rooms, Treasury of Zork

**Actors:**
- `DUNGEON-MASTER`, with a `MASTER` local-global proxy
- `OLD-MAN`
- `SHADOW` (the hooded figure; its `HOOD` and `CLOAK` are inside it)
- `MAN`, the cliff "friend", with a `GLOBAL-MAN` proxy
- `GUARDIAN` (ACTORBIT statues)
- `SAILOR` (a global; the Viking ship is `VIKING-SHIP`)
- Agents with no object: the roc, the fish, the guards and Flathead (text only), and the robot (`ROBOT-F` is only a refusal)

**Headline gaps (engine changes):**
1. **Text computed at run time** (room, item and NPC descriptions from a script, or interpolated variables). Blocks the Royal Puzzle's 3×3 diagrams (`CPWHERE`), the mirror box and hallway (`MAGIC-MIRROR`, `LOOK-TO`, `EWTELL`), and the time machine's display (`TIME-MACHINE-F` M-OBJDESC prints `TM-YEAR`).
2. **Literal arguments: numbers and quoted text.** Covers TURN/SET DIAL TO 776 (`TM-DIAL-F`), the parapet dial (`DIAL`), and SAY "FROTZ OZMOO" (`V-SAY`). The engine has no number slot, and both `turn` and `set` are built-in words that world verbs can't claim. Without this, time travel and the endgame are unplayable.
3. **Characters who obey orders** ("dungeon master, push the button / turn dial to 4 / go north / stay / follow me"). `handleOrder` says "No one obeys yet." The order rule plus script hatch can fake it, so this is B as a fallback.
4. **More prepositions in the parser:** PUT X UNDER/BEHIND Y (hiding the ring under the seat, which you must do to win), THROW X OFF/OVER Y (the ledge and the cliff), and PUSH X <direction> (Zork III's `INTDIR` parser branch, used for pushing the time machine).

Everything else is scripts (B) or data (A). Most of the work is the B column, which is large: the Royal Puzzle, the mirror box, the time-travel paradox and the shadow fight are each a Zork-I-thief-sized script.

## 2. Category C: engine gaps (ranked by how much they block)

## 3. Category B: world scripts (the code hatch)

Grouped by puzzle. "Rule" means `instead`/`after` on an item, room or NPC; "script" means `world.scripts`.

**Royal Puzzle (`CPEXIT`, `CPMOVE`, `CPGOTO`, `CPWALL-OBJECT`, `CPLADDER-*`, `CP-SLOT-FCN`, `CPENTER`, `ILLCP`).** Two possible designs:
- **(a) 36 generated rooms** `cp_1..cp_36`, with the grid in vars `cp_1..36`, using 0 (open), 1 (marble), -1 (sandstone), -2 (good ladder) and -3 (bad ladder). Orthogonal exits are data: `{to, if:'var:cp_N=0', denial:'There is a wall there.'}`. Diagonals need both side cells open, which conditions already express with `&`. Items stay in their cell, so `CPOBJS` stashing goes away.
- **(b) One room plus a stash script.**

Either way:
- PUSH <wall> (built-in `use` on the `CPEWL/CPWWL/CPNWL/CPSWL` scenery) runs a script that moves the wall, sets `CPBLOCK-FLAG` when cell 1 fills, moves things behind the wall to `CP-OUT` ("snap"), scores +1 on the first push and prints the intro text.
- Climbing the ladder at cell 1 with the good ladder next to it exits the puzzle.
- The slot takes the book (opens the steel door, sets `CP-FLAG`); anything else is atomized.
- The diagram needs C1. Design (a) changes object listing order relative to ZIL's REMOVE and re-MOVE.

**Mirror box and hallway (`MRGO`, `MIRBLOCK`, `GO-E-W`, `MIRNS`, `MIREW`, `MIRIN`, `MIROUT`, `MPANELS`, `MENDS`, `MIRMOVE`, `SHORT-POLE-F`, `MIRROR-FUNCTION`, `PANEL-FUNCTION`, `MRSWITCH`, `MREYE-ROOM`, `BEAM-FUNCTION`, `GUARDIANS`).**
- Vars `mloc`, `mdir` (0..315), `poleup` (0/1/2); flags `mr1`, `mr2`, `mirror_open`, `wood_open`, `guardians_seen`.
- Computed exits become room `instead.go` scripts that read `ctx.command.words.target` and return `{go}` or a refusal (C9 for exact semantics).
- Panel pushes rotate or slide the box, with Guardian deaths (`die`).
- Fuses: `I-MRINT` 7 and `I-PININ` 5.
- The beam: a `drop` rule in the Beam Room blocks it. BREAK/PUT/TAKE on the beam are rules. JUMP over the beam goes to `MRA`.
- Entering the Guardians' rooms is an `onEnter` death unless `flag:invis`.
- Descriptions need C1.

**Time travel (`TM-DIAL-F`, `TM-BUTTON-F`, `TGOTO`, `HAPPY-NEW-YEAR`, `MOVE-TM-OBJECTS`, `MOVE-JEWELS`, `I-SNAP`, `GUARDS-KILL`, `FLATHEAD-SENTENCE`, `OLD-TECH-MUSEUM-F`, `JEWEL-ROOM-F`, `CROWN-JEWELS-F`, `PLAQUE-F`, `TM-SEAT-F`, `TIME-MACHINE-F`).**
- Vars `year`, `tm_year`; flags `ring_concealed`, `ring_stolen`, `clumsy_robbery`, `mystery`, `machine_damaged`, `guards_present`, `tm_point`, `cleft_flag`.
- The button runs a script: it picks the outcome (death by stone below 776, guards kill, Flathead, or a jump), then:
  1. `{disembark}`
  2. moves everything in the machine and in your hands into the room you left
  3. `{go: era room, quiet}`
  4. moves the machine, the two exhibits, the plaque, the cage and the pedestal into that era
  5. `{board}`
- Fuses: `I-SNAP` 40 (snap back to `SNAP-LOC`) and `I-GUARDS-LEAVE` 3+RANDOM(12) (the script schedules it with `ctx.roll`).
- Random `onEnd` events: the robot takes the machine (4%) and the Flathead speech (6%).
- Era room variants are separate rooms (A).
- Needs C2 (dial) and C4 (ring under seat; push machine).

**The hooded figure (`SHADOW-ROOMS`, `PICK-DIRECTION`, `SHADOW-ARRIVAL`, `SHADOW-F`, `SHADOW-ATTACK`, `I-SHADOW-REPLY`, `I-CURE`, `SHADOW-DIES`, `HOOD-F`, `CLOAK-F`, `V-DIAGNOSE`).**
- This **can't use the engine's combat tables**. It is its own system: `P-STRENGTH`/`S-STRENGTH` 1..5, PROB (10×strength+10), its own message tables, and the figure hands your sword back.
- Implementation:
  - Set `world.combat: {}` so ATTACK routes through `withRules` and GWIM "(with the sword)".
  - An `instead.attack` script on the NPC.
  - A daemon script for `I-SHADOW-REPLY` (sword flies back into your hand).
  - A fuse chain for `I-CURE`.
  - Room `onEnd` scripts for appearing (30% if lit and strength > 3), "quiet footsteps" and attack mode.
  - A room `instead.go` check against a `blocked_dir` var.
- The hood comes off at strength 1 (the win). Killing the figure is a soft lock.
- DIAGNOSE is overridden through a world `capture` on the parsed `diagnose` (built-in, not wrapped in `withRules`).
- Scoring: +1 on its arrival, +1 for the first sword attack.

**The sword's glow (`I-SWORD`, `INFESTED?`).** Port Zork I's `sword_glow` script. Zork III adds the cliff and ledge special cases (`MAN-GONE`/`MAN-FLAG`) and the Guardians' rooms. Infestation counts visible ACTORBIT things in the room or in rooms one UEXIT/CEXIT/DEXIT away.

**The cliff man (`I-MAN-APPEARS`, `I-MAN-PRESENT`, `I-MAN-RETURNS`, `I-MAN-LIFT`, `I-MAN-LEAVES`, `GLOBAL-ROPE-F`, `CHEST-F`, `MAN-F`, `VALUABLES-F`, `CLIFF-F`, `CLIFF-LEDGE-F`, `CLIFF-BASE-F`).**
- A chain of fuses and flag-gated daemons: 5-turn appearance, 10-turn wait with `PICK-ONE` lines, a 10-turn return, a 4-turn lift wait, 40% leaving.
- TIE ROPE TO CHEST uses the existing world verb `tie`. Grabbing the rope sets `holding_rope`.
- He lifts you with `MOVE WINNER CLIFF`, done as `{go, quiet}`.
- Hitting him with the sword drops the valuables and breaks the staff.
- Scoring: +1 on first entering the ledge.

**The lake (`GO-ON-LAKE`, `LAKE-F`, `I-ON-LAKE`, `I-ROC`, `I-IN-LAKE`, `IN-LAKE-F`, `ON-LAKE-F`, `SHINY-OBJECT-F`).**
- Entering the lake drops everything without WEARBIT into `IN-LAKE`. In the engine that means without `onWear`, read from `world.items` in the script. The torch becomes `FRIED-TORCH` and the lamp `FRIED-LAMP`; the waybread is gone.
- Daemons: cold (warnings at 4 and 6, drowning at 8), the roc (10%, fuse 2), surfacing after 3 turns underwater.
- Underwater TAKE: a weight check and a 30% fumble.
- The shiny object becomes the amulet at 50%.
- The fish: 10% line, 4% death (`onEnd`).
- Scoring: +1 on first reaching On the Lake.
- On the Lake has RLANDBIT *and* NONLANDBIT, so you swim with no vehicle. Don't mark it `water` (that would trip `vehicleRefusal`); use a tag.

**Scenic Vista (`VIEWING-TABLE-F`, `I-VIEW-CHANGE`, `I-VIEW-SNAP`).** A self-requeuing 4-turn fuse cycles `active_view` 1..4 (queued at `GO`, so it's deterministic). RUB teleports via `{if var} go`, scores +1 the first time, and schedules the 3-turn snap. `ZORK-IV`'s `onEnter` death embeds the room text.

**Endgame (`DUNGEON-DOOR-F` KNOCK, `LOOK-LIKE-DM?`, `DMISH`, `DM-REASONS`, `I-FOLIN`, `PARAPET-F`, `DIAL`, `DIALBUTTON`, `CELL-MOVE`, `MOVE-CELL-OBJECTS`, `CELL-ROOM`, `NCELL-ROOM`, `BRONZE-DOOR-F`, `BRONZE-DOOR-EXIT`, `KEY-F`, `NIRVANA-F`).**
- KNOCK counts the seven items (amulet, book, hood, cloak, ring, key, staff). All seven: the speech, then into the dungeon. Otherwise one of seven "waiting" lines and a teleport to the Button Room.
- The cell rotation keeps a stash of each cell's objects. Use 8 offstage rooms; the script moves `CELL`'s contents out and the target cell's contents in. If you're inside, you go to `PRISON-CELL` or `GOOD-CELL` (cell 4) with the cell's contents. The bronze door is `hide`/`reveal`.
- SPIN sets the dial to `ctx.roll(8)`.
- The key works only in `GOOD-CELL`.
- The Treasury's `onEnd` runs the final text and an `end` with `score: true`.
- Needs C2 and C3 (or the B order fallback).

**The rest:**
- **Old man and secret door:** `MSTAIRS-F` 30% `onEnter`; SHAKE/WAKE wakes him (fuse 3); GIVE WAYBREAD (NPC `instead.give` or `onGive`) reveals the door; attacking him makes him vanish.
- **FROTZ OZMOO:** `V-SAY` (C2).
- **HELLO SAILOR:** a world verb `hello`, plus a rule on the `sailor` scenery while the ship is visible. The ship appears in `FLATHEAD-OCEAN-F` at 20% per turn and stays 2 turns (`I-BOAT-DISAPPEAR`).
- **Vial and potion:** DRINK sets `invis` with `I-VISIBLE` (3 turns, and death if you're among the Guardians when it wears off).
- **Earthquake:** `I-CLEFT` is queued once by `MAIN-LOOP-1` (not by `GO`, where it's commented out) at 70+RANDOM(70) after the first input, parsed or not. The script schedules with `ctx.roll`. The map change itself is A (conditional exits on `cleft_flag` and `aq_flag`; room-dependent text through `{if}`).
- **Grue repellent:** SPRAY ON ME sets `sprayed` (fuse 5). The grue variants go in `darkness.blunder`/`stumble` `{if}` steps, plus C10.
- **Lamp:** THROW gives the broken lamp; `CURRENT-LAMP` is tracked by a var or flags.
- **Death (`JIGS-UP`):** `death.then` moves the current lamp to the Endless Stair *wherever it is*, resets the strengths and the sword glow, and cancels `I-MAN-LEAVES`, `I-MAN-RETURNS`, `I-VIEW-SNAP` and `I-FOLIN`. The key goes back to the Key Room when you die in `DARK-1/2`, the Key Room or `AQ-1/2`; conditions have no OR, so that's one `variants[].before` per room or a script. In past years JIGS-UP quits outright (C11 or script).
- **PICK-ONE tables** (`YUKS`, `P-HITS`, `P-MISSES`, `S-HITS`, `S-MISSES`, `MAN-WAITS`, `GUARD-KILLERS`, `BLATHER`, `KEY-DESCS`): reuse zork1's `pickOne`. Zork III's `PICK-ONE` comes from the shared `gmacros.zil`, which differs from Zork I's only by a commented-out routine.

## 4. Category A: notable confirmations

- **Darkness and the grue:** `darkness.{look,blunder,stumble,arrive}`, `dark` rooms (no ONBIT), PROB 80 move-grue = `stumble.chance`. Grue lines specific to `DARK-1/2` go in `{if:'in:dark_1'}` steps.
- **Lamp:** `I-LANTERN` + `LAMP-TABLE` is the zork1 `lamp_fuel` var + daemons pattern (queued at 200, then 300/100/50 thresholds). The torch is `light` + `flaming`, always on.
- **Earthquake map change:** exits `{to, if:'flag:cleft_flag', denial}` (`MUSEUM-ANTE` east, `MUSEUM-ENTRANCE` west) and `{if:'flag:aq_flag'}` (`AQ-2`/`AQ-3`). Room text via `descriptions[{if}]`. The cleft is `hide`/`reveal`.
- **Doors:** iron, jewel, wooden, cell, bronze, dungeon and secret doors are `Item.door` + `container`. INVISIBLE doors use `hide`/`reveal`; exits use `door:`/`if:`.
- **Guardians:** `onEnter: [{ if:'!flag:invis', then: death }]`. Throwing at them or attacking them are rules.
- **Death framework:** `death.lives: 3` (`DEATHS > 3` is final), `respawn: 'zork2_stair'`, `resurrection`, `final`, `scatter` = `DEAD-OBJ-LOCS` with uniform `RANDOM-ELEMENT` (the same as Zork I's non-treasure branch; no `treasures:'dark'`), no penalty. `REALLY-DEAD` = an `end` with an ending that prints "**** You have died ****" and `score: true`.
- **Timers:** every `I-*` maps onto `schedule`/`cancel` fuses or flag-gated `daemons` (ZIL `QUEUE … -1` = a daemon). Random odds use `chance`. `GOTO … <>` = `{go, quiet}`; `PERFORM ,V?LOOK` = `{look}`; FCLEAR TOUCHBIT = `{unvisit}`.
- **Characters:** `moveNpc`, `npcState.hidden`, `NPC.descriptions`, `instead.give/attack/order`, `refuseGift`, `aliases`.
- **Items:** wearables (`onWear`; WEARBIT keeps them through the lake), FDESC/LDESC (`initialDescription`/`roomDescription`), READ `text` (the warning note, the seal, the repellent), PSEUDO objects as room `scenery` (mist, quicksand, torches, shore, swamp, arch, waterfall).
- **Other:** room `onEnd` for M-END flavour lines (cliff, fish, Flathead). Vehicle rules answer before the room's while aboard (`TIME-MACHINE-F` M-BEG). Existing world verbs `tie`, `untie`, `look_under`, `rub`, `listen`, `smell`, `pour`, `dig`, `jump` carry over. `carry.limit: 100` (`LOAD-MAX`). ## 5. Parser, syntax and verbs vs Zork I

The generic layer is the same code. `gmain.zil` and `gclock.zil` are identical to Zork I's. `gsyntax`, `gparser`, `gverbs`, `gglobals` and `gmacros` differ only in commented-out routines. All the real differences are `%<COND (<==? ,ZORK-NUMBER 3> …)>` branches:

- **gparser `SNARF-OBJECTS` area (around line 1053):** a direction word in adjective position becomes the `INTDIR` object with `P-DIRECTION`. This enables PUSH/MOVE/SLIDE X <direction> (C4). Zork III only.
- **gsyntax:**
  - `BUZZ FROTZ OZMOO` (so SAY reads them as raw words from `P-LEXV`).
  - HAND is not a GIVE synonym.
  - `"` is a word break (`zork3.zil` `WBREAKS`), as in Zork I.
- **gverbs:**
  - `PRE-BOARD` lets you board the channel, the seat and the lake (to their own refusals).
  - `V-CLIMB-FOO` on the rope climbs down; `V-CLIMB-ON` a vehicle climbs up.
  - LOOK IN a surface says "There is nothing on the X."
  - `PRE-MUNG` exempts the beam; `PRE-PUT` exempts the short pole.
  - `V-SAY` handles FROTZ OZMOO (only once the Dungeon Entrance has been visited, and only in the Engravings Room).
  - `V-SHAKE`'s spill tests NONLANDBIT.
  - `V-LEAP` at the Flathead Ocean has its own line.
  - `PRE-TURN` says "You should turn the X to something." for the dials and the T-bar.
  - `V-WALK` FEXIT treats `CP-MOVED` as success.
  - The grue lines in V-WALK, GOTO and V-LOOK change for `SPRAYED?` and `DARK-1/2`, and V-LOOK has the `DARK-2` line.
  - GOTO prints "The X comes to a stop." for vehicles.
  - `CRETIN-FCN` EXAMINE ME has the invisible and "pretty much as usual" texts.
  - `SAILOR-FCN` HELLO has the ship and vial branch.
  - `V-VERSION` text.
- **Overridden in 3ACTIONS:** `V-SCORE` ("potential"), `V-DIAGNOSE` (its own `DIAG` table indexed by `P-STRENGTH`), `JIGS-UP`, `LIGHT-INT` ("You'd better have more light than from the …").

Verbs Zork III leans on that the engine or the zork1 port doesn't have yet:
- **Need C2 or C4 (built-in words):** TURN/SET X TO <n>; PUT X UNDER/BEHIND Y; THROW X OFF/OVER Y; PUSH X <direction>/PUSH X TO Y.
- **Easy world verbs:** SAY (needs a literal argument, C2), HELLO [X], KNOCK ON/AT, FOLLOW, STAY, SPIN, SHAKE, WAKE/ALARM, CUT X WITH Y, FIND, SPRAY X ON Y / SPRAY Y WITH X, DRINK (FROM), EAT, SWIM/ENTER lake (V-THROUGH), CLIMB ON. WALK TO X already parses as GO.
- **Orders:** "DM, …" and "tell master to …" already parse (`orderInLine`, `orderTo`); acting on them is C3.
- **Built-in verbs that need overrides:** DIAGNOSE and SCORE (capture or C7).

## 6. Risks for differential testing against zork3.z3

- **The earthquake's timing** (`I-CLEFT` at 70+RANDOM(70), queued after the first input) gates the museum. It needs a sync point ("wait until the tremor on each side"), as the zork1 fights do.
- **Narrow random windows:**
  - The Viking ship (20% per lit turn at the ocean, gone after 2 turns; you must HELLO SAILOR in that window).
  - The old man (30% per entry).
  - The cliff man leaving (40%).
  - The guards leaving (3..15 turns).
  - **Random deaths:** guard-caught 3% per command in the old museum, fish 4% per turn underwater, the roc 10% per turn on the lake.
  - The robot taking the machine (4%).
  - The shiny object (50%).
  - Underwater TAKE fumbles (30%).
- **The shadow** appears at random (30% per M-END, lit, strength > 3), blocks a random direction (`PICK-DIRECTION` over the room's exit order, so the exit order in data must follow the story file's property order), and fights with its own dice. Check by line sets, as `zork1-fight.test.ts` does.
- **Clock order:** ZIL's `INT` allocates slots downward on *first registration*, and `CLOCKER` walks from the newest. Interrupt messages that fire in the same turn therefore come out in an order that depends on play history (`I-LANTERN` and `I-VIEW-CHANGE` from `GO`; later `I-SWORD`, `I-CURE`, `I-SHADOW-REPLY`, `I-FOLIN`, `I-ON-LAKE`, `I-IN-LAKE`, `I-ROC`). Native daemons and fuses run in a fixed order.
- **Turn counting must match exactly:**
  - `I-VIEW-CHANGE` (every 4), `I-SNAP` (40), `I-MRINT` (7), `I-PININ` (5) and the lake timers are all exact.
  - CLOCKER is skipped for meta verbs.
  - `TGOTO` adds a move.
  - `I-CLEFT` is queued even on an unparsed first line.
- **Listing order:** `CPGOTO` and `CELL-MOVE` REMOVE things and re-MOVE them, which reverses order. 36-room CP or 8-room stash designs won't reproduce that by default.
- **Game-ending paths:**
  - Dying in a past year `<QUIT>`s the VM outright.
  - `REALLY-DEAD` and the win go to FINISH's RESTART/RESTORE/QUIT prompt.
  - The harness must survive the interpreter exiting.
- **No randomness, but heavy logic:** the mirror box and cell puzzles. They're good candidates for scripted sessions compared line by line.
- **Seeding the interpreter** (ifvms xorshift, as in zork1) still applies; per-chapter seed pairs will be needed wherever the ship, shadow, man or guards matter.
